Primary Research

Speaking directly with portfolio managers and traders revealed that their biggest pain point wasn't a lack of features, but a lack of unity. When an alert fired, they needed context immediately without breaking their workflow to open a secondary app.

  • One entry point: creating, managing, and acting on an alert should never require switching tools
  • Flexible conditions: support everything from a simple price level to multi-condition, news-based triggers

Key Themes that emerged

  1. Traders get alerts for a whole desk but only care about 4 to 5 names, so they want fewer, more relevant alerts.
  2. We have no single place to catch earnings and news on a security.
  3. There’s no way to custom create our own alerts.
  4. Alerts come from the backend today, so the biggest ask is a simple way to choose which alerts you get.
  5. The alert badge keeps increasing number but nothing ever pops.
  6. The same widget has to work across both trading and research workflows, so it must stay flexible.

Ideations

Paper Prototyping & Ideation

I began by sketching low-fidelity ideas of how the widget could dock into the existing interface. The goal was to keep it unobtrusive but easily expandable when creating complex, multi-condition alerts.

Evaluation

We evaluated the initial concepts against our design principles. The winning direction relied on a sticky side-panel approach that users could toggle, ensuring they always had a clear view of their primary workspace.

Designs

The final designs focused on the core flow: pick a security (multi-select supported), set the condition and trigger, choose a priority, and create. The panel supports stacking conditions with AND/OR logic for more advanced alerts.
